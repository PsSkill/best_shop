📌 Design Notes — What Changed & Why

1 Bill number at the top — Visible and sticky throughout. Users enter it once and it stays in view so they don't forget.
2 All dropdowns on ONE card — Category, Item, Sub-Category, Brand, Model, Colour, Occasion, Type are all visible at once. Tap any dropdown to change it. Zero back navigation required. Green checkmark = filled. Grey = not yet selected.
3 Pricing on the same screen — Purchase price, Selling price, MRP are right below the product fields. No separate pricing "page".
4 Sizes & Quantities — inline table — This is the core UX fix. Instead of selecting one size and going forward, users see all sizes as rows. They just type the quantity next to each size. Adding a new size = click "+ Add Another Size". Removing = click ✕. No back step needed at all.
5 Live summary + total — Shows a running summary of what they've selected including total pieces and total value, so users can verify before submitting.
✓ Guiding principle — The whole form is ONE screen. The user fills everything on this screen and presses "Add to Stock". To add the same product in a different size, they just add a new size row. To change colour, they tap the Colour dropdown inline. Maximum 0 back steps.
